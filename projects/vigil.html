Vigil
Fall detection & live location

A free safety app for iPhone and Android. A small neural network on the phone watches for falls; if you don't answer "Are you okay?", the people in your circle get a full-screen alert with your live location.

01 / the problemThe problem

When someone falls and can't get up — an elderly parent, a person working alone, anyone — the minutes before help arrives matter. Most fall-detection solutions rely on a dedicated watch or a paid service. I wanted something that works with only the phone people already carry, costs nothing, and alerts the specific people who would actually come.

02 / what it doesWhat it does

On-device fall detectionA neural network runs on the phone itself — no audio or motion data leaves the device for detection.
"Are you okay?" countdownAfter a suspected fall, a countdown gives the person a chance to cancel a false alarm.
Full-screen circle alertsIf there's no answer, everyone in the circle gets a full-screen alert with a loud alarm and strong vibration.
SOS buttonTrigger the same alert manually at any time.
Live location & ETAHelpers tap "on my way"; the person in trouble and the circle see them approach on a map with an ETA.
Invite-link circlesStart alone, then add family by sharing a link. Circles work like groups, with admins and permissions.
Privacy controlsPer-person choices about who sees location and who receives alerts.
Movement recorderA built-in recorder to capture real phone movement for fine-tuning the model.

03 / the ml modelThe ML model

SisFall datasetAccelerometer recordings of falls and daily activities
PreprocessResampled to 50 Hz, cut into windows
1D CNNTrained in Google Colab
EvaluateSubject-independent split
TensorFlow LiteShipped inside the app

The model is evaluated subject-independently: people in the test set are never seen in training, which is closer to how it behaves for a new user.

In the app, inference is wrapped in simple logic that keeps the battery and false alarms down: the model only runs after an impact above 1.6 g, checks every 0.5 s, and has a 60-second cooldown after an alert.

HONEST STATUS

Lab datasets are recorded with sensors strapped to the body, but a phone sits in a pocket, a hand or a bag. In real use the first model raised false alarms during normal movement, so I built the recorder and a fine-tuning notebook that mixes SisFall with my own phone recordings at a low learning rate. Final evaluation numbers will be published here once the real-device data is complete.

04 / architectureArchitecture

Flutter appSensors, model, alerts, maps
Firebase AuthGoogle, email or anonymous sign-in
Cloud FirestoreCircles, members, alerts, locations — guarded by security rules
Firebase HostingInvite links: /join/CODE opens the app

Invite links are real web URLs. On Android they open the app directly through verified app links; on iPhone (without a paid Apple account) a small page offers an "Open in Vigil" button that hands the code to the app.

05 / challengesChallenges

False alarms from everyday movement

Sitting down hard or dropping the phone can look like a fall. The answer was better data from real phone placements (pocket, hand, bag, table), a fine-tuning pipeline, and the countdown so a person can cancel.

An alarm that must be heard

An alert is useless if it follows the media volume. I configured the audio session so the alarm stays loud with strong vibration even when the phone volume is turned down.

Alerts while the app is in the background

iOS limits background work. Getting alerts to arrive reliably when the app isn't open required notification and background handling specific to each platform.

Maps for everyone

The default map tiles repeated the world and showed local-language labels. I switched to tiles with English labels, limited zoom to one world, and added a satellite toggle.

06 / what i learnedWhat I learned

  • The gap between a model that scores well on a dataset and one that works in a pocket — and how to close it with data.
  • Building a real-time multi-user app on Firestore with security rules that protect location data.
  • Mobile platform realities: background limits, deep links, audio sessions, permissions.

07 / documentationDocumentation

Project layout

app/ is the Flutter app only. ml/ holds the Colab notebooks (vigil_fall_detection.ipynb, vigil_finetune.ipynb) and the exported vigil_fall_model.tflite with its config. web/ is the Firebase Hosting site for invite links. docs/ holds the architecture notes and security rules.

Run the app

  1. Install Flutter and the FlutterFire CLI.
  2. flutterfire configure with your own Firebase project (config files are not committed).
  3. flutter run on an Android emulator or a connected iPhone.

Retrain the model

Open vigil_finetune.ipynb in Colab with a T4 GPU, point it at your recordings, run all cells, and copy the new .tflite and config into app/assets/models/. No code changes are needed.
